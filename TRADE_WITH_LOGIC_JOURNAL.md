## Important Research Notes

These entries are historical observations from the journal. They should not be interpreted as proof that any setup will produce the same result in future markets. Outcomes depend on market conditions, execution, risk management, liquidity, volatility and other factors.

### Repeated themes observed in the journal

1. **Multi-timeframe confluence** appeared repeatedly in stronger setups.
2. **P / S4 / R4 levels** were frequently used as structural reference points.
3. **Gap behaviour** was repeatedly studied for continuation and reversal scenarios.
4. **HA + Normal candle confirmation** was used to validate structure.
5. **Failed setups and re-entry** were treated as learning opportunities rather than ignored.
6. **Psychology and discipline** were recurring variables, especially around FOMO, fear and over-trading.
7. **No-trade observations** were also recorded when structure was unclear or support/resistance created range conditions.
